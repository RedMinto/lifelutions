What We Offer

Comprehensive Care for Every Body

From injury recovery to preventive wellness, our multidisciplinary team delivers results-focused treatment covered by most extended health plans. Direct billing available — no paperwork hassle.

Registered Massage Therapy (RMT)
Therapeutic massage from College of Massage Therapists of Ontario (CMTO) registered practitioners. Effective for muscle tension, chronic pain, stress, and recovery from injury. Receipts provided for insurance claims.
Direct billing
Physiotherapy
Evidence-based physiotherapy to restore strength, mobility, and function. Our registered physiotherapists develop personalised treatment plans for post-surgical rehab, sports injuries, workplace injuries, and chronic conditions.
Direct billing
Chiropractic Care
Precise spinal adjustments and joint manipulation to relieve back pain, neck pain, and headaches. Our chiropractors use modern, gentle techniques to restore alignment and improve your nervous system function.
Direct billing
Acupuncture
Traditional and contemporary acupuncture for pain management, stress reduction, migraines, and overall wellness. Performed by certified practitioners using sterile, single-use needles in a relaxing environment.
Insurance covered
Custom Orthotics
Prescription custom foot orthotics designed to correct biomechanical imbalances, relieve foot, knee, and lower back pain. Gait analysis and casting performed on-site, covered by most extended health plans.
Direct billing
Sports Injury Rehabilitation
Get back in the game faster. Our sports rehab program combines physiotherapy, manual therapy, and exercise programming to recover from sprains, strains, ligament injuries, and overuse conditions at any fitness level.
All skill levels

About Us

A Team That Truly Cares About Your Recovery

Body First Wellness Centre has served the Scarborough community for years, building a reputation for exceptional, patient-centred care. Located conveniently at 4865 Sheppard Ave E, our clinic brings together registered massage therapists, physiotherapists, chiropractors, and acupuncturists under one roof.

We believe healing is not a one-size-fits-all journey. Every treatment plan is tailored to your unique needs, goals, and lifestyle — whether you're managing a workplace injury, recovering from surgery, dealing with chronic pain, or simply investing in your long-term wellness.
