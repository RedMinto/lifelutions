What We Do

Full-Service Auto Repair
& Tire Centre

From your engine to your exhaust, our licensed mechanics handle it all — with honest diagnostics and upfront pricing every time.

🛑

Brakes

Squealing, grinding, or a soft pedal? We inspect and replace brake pads, rotors, calipers, and lines to keep you stopping safely.

🔄

Tires & Wheel Alignment

New tires from top brands, expert mounting and balancing, and precise computerized alignment for even wear and better fuel economy.

🛢️

Oil Change

Conventional, synthetic blend, or full synthetic — quick in-and-out oil changes with a multi-point inspection included at no extra charge.

🔍

Engine Diagnostics

Check engine light on? Our technicians use advanced scan tools to pull codes and pinpoint the root cause fast — no guesswork, no unnecessary repairs.

🚗

Suspension & Steering

Shocks, struts, ball joints, tie rods — we restore ride comfort and handling precision so your car drives the way it should.

⚡

Battery & Electrical

Dead battery, faulty alternator, or wiring issues? We test, diagnose, and replace electrical components with same-day turnaround in most cases.

📋

Safety Inspection

Ministry of Transportation–certified safety inspections for used vehicle sales, licensing, or peace of mind — completed by certified technicians.

❄️

A/C Service

Recharge refrigerant, replace compressors, fix leaks — we get your air conditioning blowing cold again before Scarborough's summer heat hits hard.
