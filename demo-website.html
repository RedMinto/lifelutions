What We Do

Electrical Services for Homes & Businesses

Whether you're renovating, adding a new circuit, or dealing with an urgent electrical issue, our licensed team handles it safely and to code — every time.

⚡

Panel Upgrades

Replace an outdated 100-amp panel with a modern 200-amp or 400-amp service. Eliminate fire risks from old fuse boxes and get the capacity your home or business demands.

🔌

EV Charger Installation

Certified Level 2 EV charger installation (240V) for all vehicle makes. We handle the permit, inspection, and installation — you just plug in and charge overnight.

💡

Lighting & Pot Lights

Recessed pot lights, LED upgrades, dimmer switches, exterior lighting, and full commercial lighting designs. Brighten your space and cut your hydro bill at the same time.

🔧

Wiring & Rewiring

New construction rough-in, basement finishing, kitchen and bathroom circuits, knob-and-tube replacement, and aluminum wiring remediation — done right the first time.

🚨

Emergency Repairs

Power outages, tripped breakers that won't reset, burning smells, or sparking outlets — our emergency team is available 7 days a week with a fast response across the GTA.

📋

Electrical Inspections

Pre-purchase home inspections, ESA compliance certificates, and landlord safety inspections. We provide clear written reports so you can buy, sell, or rent with confidence.
