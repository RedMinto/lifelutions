Our Story

Rooted in Ontario.
Served with intention.

🌿
Est. 2018 · Kensington Market

Cedar & Sage Bistro was born from a simple belief: that the best meal you'll ever eat starts long before the kitchen. It starts at a farm. We opened our doors in 2018 in the heart of Kensington Market, and from day one we've built every menu around what Ontario's farmers are growing that week.

Our executive chef, Marisol Vega, spent years cooking at farm-to-table restaurants in Vancouver and Vermont before bringing her philosophy to Toronto — a city she believes is underrated for its extraordinary local food culture. Every plate that leaves our kitchen is a collaboration between Marisol's team and the growers we've built real relationships with over six years.

We're a cozy spot. Forty-two seats, warm lighting, and a kitchen that's always a little bit open. Come as you are. Stay as long as you like.
